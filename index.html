The Problem

Spreadsheets don't
warn you.

Travel agency ops teams still track driver, vehicle, guide, and room schedules in spreadsheets or paper registers. Nothing checks a new booking against what's already on the books — so the same driver or vehicle gets assigned to two trips on the same day, and nobody notices until someone's standing at the pickup point with no ride.

  • 01 Clashes are discovered the day of the trip, not the day they're booked.
  • 02 Re-checking the whole register by hand doesn't scale during peak season.
  • 03 Finding a replacement driver or vehicle under pressure means more mistakes, not fewer.

How It Works

Checked the moment it's entered.

Every booking runs through a straightforward date-range overlap check against existing bookings for that same resource — no ML, no black box, just a rule anyone can audit.

01
📝

Staff enters a booking

Resource type, resource, trip name, customer, and date range — the same fields a spreadsheet row would have.

⚡

Instant clash detection

startA ≤ endB && startB ≤ endA against every existing booking for that resource.

→

Confirmed, or fixed

No clash → booked instantly. Clash → staff sees exactly what it collides with and a free alternative in one click.

Features

What's actually implemented

Everything below is running in the live demo — not slideware. Nothing here is claimed as done unless you can click it.

◆

Live clash-detection board

Every resource against the coming week, color-coded Open / Booked / Conflict, filterable by type and searchable by name or trip.

⚠

Conflict modal + suggestion

Shows the exact colliding booking(s) and the nearest free alternative resource — switch, force-book, or cancel in one click.

▤

Timeline view

A Gantt-style week view per resource, for staff who think in bars on a calendar rather than a grid of cells.

⌘

Command palette

⌘K for instant search across every action — filter, export, jump weeks, undo, print — without touching a menu.

▦

Analytics panel

Bookings by resource type, utilization by type, and a clash trend line for the visible window — all live, no ML.

↺

Undo, export, print

Full undo stack for every change, one-click CSV export, and a print-ready stylesheet for the schedule board.
